Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the PregnaWheel mobile application ("Application"), which is available on the market as a digital pregnancy wheel. The Application is intended to help users estimate pregnancy-related dates for informational purposes only.


1. Overview

PregnaWheel is designed as a simple informational tool. It does not require account creation, and it is designed to operate without collecting, storing, or transmitting personal information to the developer.


2. Informational Use Only

PregnaWheel is provided for educational and informational use only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical monitoring. You should always seek guidance from a qualified healthcare professional regarding pregnancy, health concerns, or medical decisions.


3. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, store, process, or share personal information. No registration, login, phone number, email address, or account is required to use the Application. Calculations are performed locally on your device.


4. Dates and Health-Related Inputs

The Application may allow you to enter dates, such as the last menstrual period or other pregnancy-related reference dates, in order to perform calculations. This information is used only on your device for the requested calculation and is not transmitted to the developer or third parties. The Application is not intended to create, maintain, or share a medical record.


5. Automatically Collected Data

The Application is designed not to automatically collect analytics data, usage statistics, crash logs linked to you, advertising identifiers, precise location, or other tracking information.


6. Permissions

PregnaWheel does not require access to sensitive device features such as location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, or call logs. If a future version introduces new features that require permissions, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes take effect.


7. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Because the Application does not collect personal information, it does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your data with third parties. The Application is not intended to use advertising networks or third-party analytics services to profile users.


8. Data Retention

The developer does not retain user data because the Application does not collect it. If any information is temporarily processed on your device during use, it remains under your control on that device only.


9. Your Choices

You may stop using the Application at any time by uninstalling it from your device. Because no account is created and no personal data is collected by the developer, there is no server-side user profile or stored account information to delete.


10. Children’s Privacy

PregnaWheel does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since the Application does not collect personal data from any users, the privacy risk to children is limited.


11. Security

Because the Application is designed not to collect or transmit personal data, the risk of developer-side exposure of personal information is reduced. However, you remain responsible for the security of your own device and any information you manually enter while using the Application.


12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect legal, technical, or product changes. Any updated version will be posted with a revised effective date.


13. Effective Date

This policy is effective as of April 14, 2026.
