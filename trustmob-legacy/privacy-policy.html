Privacy Policy — TrustMob Companion

Effective date: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bladroidx (“we”, “us”) handles information in the TrustMob Companion Android app (package com.bladroidx.app.legacy.phonecheck, “the companion app”).

Summary: the companion app does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • It is used only to run diagnostic tests on the phone it is installed on, and to send the result to the TrustMob Check app you pair it with.
  • It has no account, no server, and never sends anything to us or anyone else over the internet.
  • It saves nothing: when you close it, the report is gone.
  • It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

1. What the companion app does

You install the companion app on a phone you want to check, typically a phone being bought or sold. It tests that phone’s hardware and software (screen, cameras, sensors, battery, storage, connectivity and similar) and shows the results. When you pair it with a phone running TrustMob Check, it sends the diagnostic report to that phone. It does nothing else.

2. Data we collect

None. The companion app has no account, sign-in or registration, and it has no server of its own. It never sends data to us, and it does not access your personal data: it does not read your contacts, messages, call logs, photos, videos, audio, files, accounts, calendar, browsing history, location history or the phone’s IMEI.

3. Data stored on the phone

None. The diagnostic report exists only in memory while the app is open. It cannot be saved, exported or printed from the companion app, and it is gone when you close the app. The storage-speed test writes a temporary file to measure read and write speed and deletes it immediately. The app is excluded from Android backup, so nothing is copied to a cloud backup.

4. Sending the report to TrustMob Check

The report is sent only when you pair with a phone running TrustMob Check, and only to that phone. It travels directly between the two phones over Google Nearby Connections, a local Bluetooth / Wi-Fi link; it does not go over the internet or through our servers.

The report contains the phone’s model, hardware and software facts (such as Android version, storage, memory and battery details) and the result of each test. It contains no personal data. Once received, it is handled under the TrustMob Check Privacy Policy.

5. Device permissions

Each permission is requested only when needed, used only while the app is open, and used only for a test or for pairing. Nothing it gives access to is recorded, stored or sent.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data from the companion app, because it collects none. The only thing that leaves the phone is the diagnostic report, sent directly to the TrustMob Check phone you choose to pair with.

7. Third-party SDKs

The companion app uses Google Play services — Nearby Connections to pair with TrustMob Check. Google Play services include Google’s diagnostics and transport library, which may send device diagnostic data to Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not access the advertising ID.

8. Security

Because the companion app keeps no data, there is nothing stored to protect. A link to another phone is set up only when a TrustMob Check phone scans the one-time pairing code shown on the companion app’s screen, and Nearby Connections encrypts the data it carries.

9. Retention and deletion

The companion app retains no data, so there is nothing to access, download or delete. Closing the app discards the report; uninstalling the app removes it completely.

10. Children

The companion app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above when we do.
