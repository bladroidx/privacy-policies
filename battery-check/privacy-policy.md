# BatteryCheck Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026

## 1. Overview
BatteryCheck is designed to analyze and display battery information directly on your device. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All battery information is processed locally on your device.

## 2. Information We Collect
BatteryCheck does not collect any personally identifiable information.
The app only reads system-provided battery data, such as:

- Battery level
- Battery health
- Charging status and speed
- Temperature
- Voltage
- Estimated time to full or empty

This information is accessed only for display and is never transmitted to any server.

## 3. Permissions
BatteryCheck uses standard Android system APIs to read battery status.
The app does not request or require sensitive permissions such as:

- Location
- Contacts
- Camera
- Microphone
- Storage

## 4. Data Storage and Security
BatteryCheck does not store any user data on the device or externally.
All calculations and readings occur in real time and remain on your device.

## 5. Third-Party Services
BatteryCheck does not use:

- Analytics services
- Advertising SDKs
- Tracking technologies
- Third-party data processors

## 6. Children's Privacy
BatteryCheck does not collect any data and is safe for all ages.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect improvements or legal requirements.
Any changes will be published within the app and on the app's listing page.

## 8. Contact
For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the support email listed in the app's Play Store page.
