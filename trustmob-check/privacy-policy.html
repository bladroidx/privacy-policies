Privacy policy

Effective 27 September 2026

This policy explains what TrustMob Check (also published as El ThiqaMob) (“the app”) and its companion app TrustMob Companion (outside Google Play, the El ThiqaMob companion), package com.bladroidx.app.legacy.phonecheck (“the companion app”), both published by Bladroidx, collect, why, and what you can do about it. The app checks a used phone: it runs diagnostic tests on the phone itself, and can look up an IMEI to see whether a phone is reported lost, stolen or blocked. The companion app is installed on a phone being checked so its report can be sent to the app.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and, if you set one, a display name. Your password is stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Your signed-in devices: when you sign in, the app sends the device name (manufacturer and model) and the app’s user agent, so you can recognise and sign out your sessions under Account.
  • IMEI numbers you check: an IMEI you type, scan or paste for a clean check is sent to our server to run the check against third-party databases. On the phone, the app keeps only the last 4 digits. If you are signed in, our server keeps your clean-check history (a masked IMEI, a keyed hash of it, and the result) so you can see it again.
  • Diagnostic reports, only if you turn on report sync (a Pro feature, off by default): the report’s device model and build facts and the results of each check.
  • Purchases: records of credit packs and Pro subscriptions bought through Google Play Billing (product, order id, status). We never see your card or payment details — Google handles those.
  • Emails we send you: email-verification and password-reset messages, sent to your account address.
  • Server logs: like any web service, our servers record technical request details (IP address, time and request path) for security and troubleshooting, kept for a limited period.

What we do not collect

  • Camera images, microphone audio and location. Some diagnostic tests use the camera, microphone or GPS to check that they work; that happens on the phone only and is never uploaded.
  • No advertising ID, no ads, and no selling or renting of your data.

The companion app

The companion app has no account and sends nothing to our servers. It runs diagnostic tests on the phone being checked and sends the resulting report only to the paired TrustMob Check (also published as El ThiqaMob) phone, directly over Google Nearby Connections (Bluetooth / Wi-Fi, locally, not through our servers).

In both apps, the camera, the microphone (used by the main app only), location and sensors are used on the device only, for the tests, and are never uploaded. Location permission is used to test GPS and, on older Android versions, is required by the system to discover nearby devices for pairing.

Third-party services

  • Google Play services — Nearby Connections, to pair the app with the companion app.
  • Google Play Billing, for purchases. Payments are processed by Google under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Google’s diagnostics and transport library, which Google Play services include, may send device diagnostics to Google.

We use no advertising or analytics SDKs and do not access the advertising ID.

Why we use it

Only to run the app’s features for you: your account and sign-in, clean checks, your history, report sync, purchases and credits, account emails, and keeping the service secure.

Who we share it with

  • The IMEI-check service and its data suppliers, to answer a clean check.
  • Google Play, to verify and manage purchases.
  • Our hosting and email-delivery providers, who process data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or your account. You can delete individual items, and set how long history is kept, in the app’s settings. Deleting your account signs you out everywhere and erases your clean-check history, synced reports, settings and profile. We keep purchase and credit records, which contain no phone data, because we need them for refunds, disputes and tax; your email address stays reserved so a deleted account cannot be re-registered by someone else. Deleted data may remain in backups for a limited period until they are overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data in the app: Account → Download my data.
  • Delete your account in the app, or on the web at /account/delete.
  • Correct your display name and email in the app, or contact us for anything else, including access, correction or objection requests under the law that applies to you.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with a memory-hard algorithm, and the server stores IMEIs only in masked and hashed form.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
