Privacy Policy — TrustMob Check

Effective date: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bladroidx (“we”, “us”) handles your information when you use the TrustMob Check Android app (package com.bladroidx.app.phonecheck, “the app”) and its online service. It describes what data the app accesses, collects, uses and shares, why, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

Our companion app, TrustMob Companion, has its own privacy policy.

Summary

  • You can use the diagnostic tests without an account. An account is needed only for clean checks, history, sync and purchases.
  • Camera, microphone, location and sensors are used on your phone only, for the tests. They are never recorded or uploaded.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, use analytics or advertising SDKs, or access your advertising ID.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or at /account/delete.

1. What the app does

The app helps you check a used phone before you buy or sell it. It runs diagnostic tests on the phone’s hardware and software, can receive a diagnostic report from another phone running TrustMob Companion, and can look up an IMEI to see whether a phone has been reported lost, stolen or blocked (a “clean check”).

3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect your location, photos, videos, audio recordings, contacts, messages, call logs, calendar, files, browsing history, or the IMEI of your own phone. We do not collect payment card details: Google Play handles payments.

5. Reports from TrustMob Companion

When you pair with a phone running TrustMob Companion, its diagnostic report is sent directly to your phone over Google Nearby Connections, a local Bluetooth / Wi-Fi link that does not go through our servers. The report is then treated like one made on your own phone: it stays on your phone unless you turn on report sync.

6. How we share data

We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and we do not share it for advertising. We pass data only to the following, and only for the purposes above:

  • IMEI lookup providers, which receive the IMEI you check in order to answer a clean check. They act as our service providers.
  • Google Play, to process and verify purchases and subscriptions.
  • Hosting and email-delivery providers, which store data and send account emails on our behalf and may not use it for anything else.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or others.

If our business is ever transferred, your data would move with it under this policy, and we would tell you before it is handled differently.

7. Third-party SDKs

  • Google Play services — Nearby Connections, to pair with TrustMob Companion.
  • Google Play Billing Library, for purchases.
  • Google Play services include Google’s diagnostics and transport library, which may send device diagnostic data to Google.

These are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

8. Security

  • All data sent between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS only).
  • Passwords are stored only as salted, memory-hard hashes (Argon2id).
  • IMEIs are stored only masked and as a keyed hash, never in full.
  • Sign-in sessions are short-lived and can be signed out from the app at any time.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we protect your data with measures appropriate to its sensitivity.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account, clean-check history and synced reports: until you delete them or your account. You can choose how long history is kept in the app’s settings.
  • Purchase and credit records: kept after account deletion, as required for refunds, disputes, accounting and tax. They contain no phone data.
  • Server logs: kept for a limited period for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in backups for a limited period until they are overwritten.

10. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Account & settings → Delete account.
  • On the web, without the app: /account/delete.

Deleting your account signs you out on every device and permanently erases your profile, clean-check history, synced reports and settings. As explained above, we keep purchase and credit records, and your email address stays reserved so no one else can register it. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription: cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

You can also delete individual history items and synced reports in the app without deleting your account.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, download, delete or restrict the use of your data, or to object to how we use it. You can download your data in the app (Account → Download my data), change your display name and email in the app, and delete your data as described above. For any other request, contact us at the address below. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. International processing

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country. Wherever your data is processed, it is protected as described in this policy.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above, and where the law requires it we will notify you or ask for your consent before a significant change applies.
